    The road to Vice Got a Price!

    One playthrough is enough, no online play is needed, and there is no mission replay — so keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (the game gives you 15 manual slots). PowerPyx rates the set 4/10 in difficulty and about 200 hours to the platinum: 60–80 hours of story, about 100 hours on the 100% checklist, and about 10 hours of cleanup.

    1. Stage 1 — Play the story 60–80 hours · Chapters 1–5

      Finish all five chapters and the story trophies pop on their own: one per chapter, one for the final mission, and one each for Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child’s Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown and Operation Savage Glory. Along the way, do the optional missions as they appear, scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app, study animals with binoculars, and start the side-character questlines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) the moment they are offered. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation for Positive Mental Attitude before Chapter 1 ends — it is the only missable trophy.

    2. Stage 2 — The 100% checklist about 100 hours · after the credits

      After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. The list is lenient — closer to 60% of true completion: scan 200 of 277 base-model vehicles, study 100 of 255 animals, finish any 70 of 80 challenges, and clear every robbery, shooting range, drone race and Bret quest. Progress made during the story already counts. This stage earns the gold A Long Road and most of the silver.

    3. Stage 3 — Cleanup about 10 hours · free-roam

      Finish the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands, the two still-unconfirmed trophies (Bad Luck, I Guess and Ten Pin — launch-week guides are still pinning them down), and anything left on your tracker. When the last trophy pops, the platinum Vice Got a Price! pops with it.

    What the 100% checklist asks for

    • 200 of 277 base-model vehicles scanned with WAiNK
    • 100 of 255 animals studied with binoculars
    • Any 70 of the 80 challenges
    • All 8 large bank vaults, all 39 illegal gang operations, all 12 shooting ranges, all 14 Top Fun drone-race locations, all 15 Bret Target Decks
    • 15 of the 18 meditation spots — so the missable Chapter 1 meditation cannot cost you A Long Road

    Counts from PowerPyx’s GTA 6 trophy guide and 100% completion guide.

    Save habits that protect your run

    • Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter — there is no mission replay and no New Game+.
    • Do the Chapter 1 meditation the morning after the mission Down to Earth.
    • Cheats switch off trophies and saving — keep them off for the whole run.
    • Don’t push your Criminal Profile to Irredeemable: it is permanent and police turn far more aggressive.
